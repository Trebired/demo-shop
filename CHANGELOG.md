# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Added the demo shop for Okrová dílna, a fictional ceramics workshop: catalog, product pages, cart, checkout, order pages with bank transfer and its QR code, customer accounts and password reset, in Czech and English, all from the `@trebired/e-shop` storefront preset.
- The shop has its own tbf design: warm paper palette, terracotta accent, Fraunces and Figtree, light first with a dark mode.
- Bitcoin payments through BTCPay Server turn on with their credentials.
- Card payments, Packeta and order email turn on when their credentials are set.
- The home texts, the checkout note and the footer are edited through the content management in `@trebired/frontend`.
